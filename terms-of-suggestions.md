# Terms of Suggestions

These are the guidelines for what kinds of suggestions are welcome in the CJP Community Platform.

Please read this before submitting any suggestion to the platform.

---

## ✅ What You CAN Suggest

* A **new principle or value** that you feel is missing from the Code of Conduct
* **Simpler or clearer wording** for an existing point
* **Better structure** — for example, grouping related ideas together
* **Examples or explanations** that make a point easier to understand for new readers
* **Improvements to community processes** that strengthen our platform
* **Suggestions for new features** that improve transparency and participation

---

## ❌ What You CANNOT Suggest

* **Removing a point** without providing a strong, logical reason
* Changes that make the Code of Conduct target a **specific person, group, or political party**
* Anything that goes against the **spirit of the Code of Conduct** — respect, accountability, openness, empathy
* **Hate speech, discrimination, or harassment** of any kind
* **Misinformation or disinformation** designed to mislead
* **Commercial spam** or self-promotion
* **Personal attacks, doxxing, or threats** against any individual or group
* Content that **violates intellectual property rights**
* **Attempts to circumvent platform rules** or security measures

---

## Language Guidelines

Write in **simple, clear language**.

Ask yourself: *Can a community member read this and understand it immediately?*
If yes — it's good. If no — simplify it.

Avoid jargon, unnecessarily complicated sentences, or language that could be misunderstood.

---

## Constructive Feedback

Each suggestion should include:

* **Clear title** of what you're proposing
* **Current text** (if modifying existing CoC section)
* **Proposed change** with your wording
* **Reasoning** explaining why this change improves the CoC

---

## Community Voting

All community members can vote on suggestions using upvote reactions (👍).

* Voting is **anonymous** — votes cannot be traced to your identity
* Higher vote count shows stronger community support
* **Moderators review** suggestions along with vote counts
* Final decision rests with moderators, not vote count alone

---

## Moderation Process

**Pending**: Your suggestion is awaiting moderator review

**Accepted**: Merged into the official Code of Conduct

**Rejected**: Declined with documented reason stored in the repository

---

## When a Suggestion Gets Rejected

Rejection is not personal.

Every rejection will come with a **clear, stated reason** in the issue — no suggestion will be dismissed without explanation.

If you disagree with a rejection, you can:

* **Comment in the issue** to request clarification or discussion
* **Appeal** by opening a new suggestion with additional reasoning
* **Contact moderators** through community channels

All reasoning is public and auditable.

---

## Appeals Process

If you believe your suggestion was unfairly rejected:

1. Comment in the original issue explaining your concern
2. Provide additional evidence or reasoning
3. Request that a different moderator review your appeal
4. Allow time for community discussion

Appeals are reviewed by a different moderator to ensure fairness.

---

## Data Retention

All suggestions, including rejected ones, are stored permanently in the GitHub repository.

* **Accepted suggestions** are merged into the Code of Conduct
* **Rejected suggestions** remain visible with rejection reasons
* **Pending suggestions** remain open until moderator decision
* **All data is public, immutable, and auditable** via git history

---

## Prohibited Content Summary

The following will result in permanent removal and possible platform ban:

- Hate speech or discriminatory language targeting any group
- Personal attacks, harassment, or doxxing
- Threats, incitement to violence, or illegal content
- Sexual or explicit content
- Spam or commercial self-promotion
- Misinformation designed to mislead
- Intellectual property violations
- Coordinated harassment campaigns
- Attempts to manipulate or exploit platform systems

---

## Final Note

By submitting a suggestion, you agree to:

* Follow these Terms of Suggestions
* Respect the Code of Conduct
* Accept moderation decisions respectfully
* Contribute constructively to community dialogue

This platform thrives through good-faith participation. Bad-faith behavior undermines our shared mission.
