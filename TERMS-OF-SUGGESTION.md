# Terms of Suggestion

These are the guidelines for what kinds of suggestions are welcome in this repository.

Please read this before opening a Pull Request.

---

## ✅ What You CAN Suggest

* A **new principle or value** that you feel is missing from the CoC
* **Simpler or clearer wording** for an existing point
* **Better structure** — for example, grouping related ideas together
* **Examples or explanations** that make a point easier to understand for a new reader

---

## ❌ What You CANNOT Suggest

* **Removing a point** without providing a strong, logical reason
* Changes that make the CoC target a **specific person, group, or political party**
* Anything that goes against the **spirit of the CoC** — respect, accountability, openness, empathy
* Suggestions about **other CJP projects or campaigns** — this repo is only for the Code of Conduct

---

## Language Guidelines

Write in  **simple, clear English** .

Ask yourself: *Can a 15-year-old read this and understand it immediately?*
If yes — it's good. If no — simplify it.

Avoid jargon, complicated sentences, or language that could be misunderstood.

---

## One Idea Per PR

Please keep each Pull Request focused on  **a single change or addition** .

This makes it much easier for everyone to discuss, review, and decide on your suggestion fairly.

Multiple unrelated changes in one PR will be asked to be split up before review.

---

## When a Suggestion Gets Rejected

Rejection is not personal.

Every rejection will come with a **clear, stated reason** — no suggestion will be silently dismissed.

If you disagree with a rejection, you may open a discussion in the PR comments. All reasoning is public.

---

*These terms exist to keep the CoC clean, focused, and fair for everyone.*
