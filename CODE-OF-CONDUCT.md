# CJP Community — Code of Conduct

### Draft v1

---

## 1. Start With Yourself

Hold yourself to the same standards you expect from others.
Before questioning the government or anyone else, honestly look at your own thoughts, habits, and actions.

Real change starts at the individual level — not as a condition for speaking up, but as the foundation of it.

---

## 2. Think Before You Speak

Be thoughtful with your words — here in this community and in daily life.

Disagreement is welcome. Disrespect is not.
Staying calm and logical when you disagree is a sign of strength, not weakness.

---

## 3. Know What You're Talking About

Build your opinions on facts and reasoning — not on emotion or things you've heard repeated many times.

Before joining a discussion, make a genuine effort to understand the topic.
A well-informed voice is always more powerful than a loud one.

---

## 4. Be Open to Changing Your Mind

Share your views with confidence. Defend them with logic. Be genuinely open when someone offers better reasoning.

Changing your mind when you learn something new is not a defeat — it is growth.

---

## 5. Be a Responsible Citizen

Being a good citizen is not only about protests or politics.

It lives in how you treat shared spaces, how you behave in your community, and how much effort you put into understanding the systems you want to change.

---

## 6. Spread the Right Mindset

Be a good example — in your family, friend group, workplace, and neighbourhood.

Good ideas spread from person to person. One mindful person can influence ten, who each influence ten more.

---

## 7. Engage With Respect and Empathy

Anger at injustice is valid and real.

But lasting change needs patience, empathy, and a genuine desire to bring people along — including those who disagree with you.

Anger can open a door. Understanding is what walks through it.
