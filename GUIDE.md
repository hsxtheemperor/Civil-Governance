# How to Contribute

Thank you for wanting to help improve this Code of Conduct.
Here is a simple step-by-step guide on how to do it.

---

## Before You Begin

* Read `CODE-OF-CONDUCT.md` fully — understand what is already there
* Read `TERMS-OF-SUGGESTION.md` — check whether your idea fits the guidelines
* Make sure your suggestion does not already exist in a different form

---

## Steps to Suggest a Change

### Step 1 — Fork this repository

Click the **Fork** button at the top right of this page.
This creates your own copy of the repo where you can make changes safely.

### Step 2 — Make your changes

In your fork, edit the relevant file (usually `CODE-OF-CONDUCT.md`).
Keep your changes focused —  **one idea per contribution** .

### Step 3 — Open a Pull Request (PR)

Come back to this repository and open a  **Pull Request** .

In your PR, clearly write:

* **What** you are adding or changing (one sentence)
* **Why** you think it improves the CoC
* Any **examples** or context that support your suggestion

### Step 4 — Join the discussion

Your PR will be open for public discussion. Be ready to:

* Explain your reasoning calmly
* Listen to feedback with an open mind
* Revise your suggestion if needed

A PR can only be  **rejected with a clearly stated reason** .
All discussion is public and permanently recorded.

---

## Important Notes

* All contributions must themselves follow the Code of Conduct
* Be respectful in discussions — this is a civic community, not a debate club
* If you are new to GitHub and need help, ask in the CJP Discord server

---

*One mindful contribution can influence the whole community.*
